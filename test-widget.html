Restaurant Chatbot Widget Test

This page demonstrates how the restaurant chatbot widget will appear on a client's website.

The widget should appear as a floating chat button in the bottom-right corner. Click it to open the chat interface.

You can test the chatbot by asking questions like:

  • "What are your hours?"
  • "Do you have vegetarian options?"
  • "What's your most popular dish?"
  • "Can I make a reservation?"
