🧪 Widget Testing Instructions

Follow these steps to test the chatbot widget functionality:

How to Test the Widget:

  1. Look for the chat button - You should see a blue floating button in the bottom-right corner
  2. Click the button - This will open the chat widget
  3. Try these test questions:
    • "What's on your menu?"
    • "What are your hours?"
    • "Do you have vegetarian options?"
    • "How much does the Chicken Tikka Masala cost?"
    • "What's your most popular dish?"
  4. Test responsiveness - Try resizing your browser window or testing on mobile
  5. Test closing/reopening - Click the button again to close and reopen the widget

Expected Results:

  • ✅ Chat widget opens smoothly
  • ✅ AI responds with restaurant-specific information
  • ✅ Brand colors are applied correctly
  • ✅ Widget works on different screen sizes
  • ✅ Professional, polished appearance
